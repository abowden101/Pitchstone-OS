# Pitchstone Branding

## Logo Direction

Abstract geometric crystalline / mineral form. Sharp, modern, premium.

- Primary mark: Faceted stone/crystal shape
- Accent: Cool blue (#5B8CFF) used sparingly for energy and precision
- Works in monochrome and full color
- Should feel at home next to high-end tech and security brands

First concept generated and stored for review.

Clean geometric sans (Inter / similar). "Pitchstone" or "Pitchstone OS".
